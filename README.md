# Cardoku 🚗

Park one car in every row, column and colour zone. Cars can't touch, not even at the corners.

- Endless levels. It starts on tiny 4×4 boards and gets harder very slowly. Some levels are easy, some are tough.
- Early levels have one-square zones that give you a free car, and can be solved just by spotting zones with one square left.
- Every 10th level is a big **HARD MODE**.
- You get 3 hearts. A wrong car costs one. Lose them all and you restart the same level.
- Tap once for ✕, twice for a car. Drag to mark lots of ✕s.
- Works offline and can be added to your phone's home screen.

Car photos by @noahshootscars_

## Put it online with GitHub Pages

1. On github.com, click **+** → **New repository**, name it `cardoku`, set it **Public**, click **Create repository**.
2. Click **uploading an existing file** and drag in these 9 files:
   `index.html`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `car.png`, `car1.jpg`, `car2.jpg`, `README.md`
3. Click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**. Under *Branch* pick `main` and `/ (root)`, click **Save**.
5. Wait a minute, then open `https://<your-username>.github.io/cardoku/` on your phone.

## Add it to your home screen

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share button → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**
